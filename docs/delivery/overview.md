# Delivery Partner Role — Overview & Fleet Operations

The **Delivery Partner** role connects rural farm gates to urban customer doorsteps through cold-chain logistics.

## Core Delivery Capabilities
1. **Available Pickups Pool**: Discover orders marked `READY_FOR_PICKUP` across nearby farms.
2. **Atomic Order Claiming (Instruction 53)**: Atomically claim farm pickups with zero race-condition collisions.
3. **Active Trip Workflow**: Progress deliveries through `PICKED_UP`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, and `DELIVERED`.
4. **Real-time GPS & WebSocket Broadcasting**: Keep customers informed with live status events.
5. **Earning Settlements**: Instant per-delivery payout credited directly to the partner wallet.
