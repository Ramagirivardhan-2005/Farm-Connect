# Subscriptions & Channel Pinning

Routes: `/customer/subscribers`

## Subscribing to Channels (Instruction 19)
Customers can subscribe to any verified farmer channel with a single click.
- Subscribing grants access to priority harvest notifications and flash harvest offers.
- Subscribed channels populate the promotional banner feed on the customer dashboard.

## Channel Pinning (Instruction 20)
- Customers can pin their favorite channels.
- Pinned channels are dynamically hoisted to appear first on that customer's dashboard and search views.
- **Privacy & Integrity Invariant**: Pinning is strictly personal and does NOT distort the public ranking or rating calculations of the channel for other users.
