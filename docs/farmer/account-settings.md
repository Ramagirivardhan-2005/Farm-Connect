# Farmer Account & Farm Profile Settings

Route: `/farmer/settings`

## Settings Overview
Farmers configure farm operational parameters:
- **Farm Location & Geocoordinates**: Accurate farm gate coordinates for delivery riders.
- **Harvest Cutoff Times**: Daily morning order cutoff (e.g., 6:00 AM) after which orders shift to the next harvest day.
- **Bank Settlement Details**: Payout account updates require mandatory dual-factor OTP verification.
- **Notification Channels**: SMS, Email, and Push alerts for new incoming orders and low inventory warnings.
