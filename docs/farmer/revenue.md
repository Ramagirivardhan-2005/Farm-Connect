# Farmer Revenue & Financial Payouts

Route: `/farmer/revenue`

## Revenue Architecture
FarmConnect operates on a transparent farm settlement model:
- **Direct Payouts**: 95-98% of product gross value settles directly to the farmer's bank account.
- **Escrow Release**: Payment funds are held in escrow and released automatically upon customer delivery confirmation.
- **Ledger Records**: Every order payout is recorded with an immutable transaction identifier.
- **Zero Frontend Derivation**: Financial summaries and daily earnings charts are generated exclusively from verified database transaction ledgers.
