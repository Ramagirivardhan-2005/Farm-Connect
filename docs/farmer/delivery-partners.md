# Delivery Partner System for Farmers

Route: `/farmer/delivery-partners`

## Partner Network Management (Instruction 50 & 51)
Farmers coordinate with verified local delivery riders to pick up packed orders from the farm gate:
- **Add Delivery Partner**: Full Legal Name, Phone Number, Vehicle Type (Electric Scooter, Cargo Van, Motorcycle), Vehicle License Plate, Service Radius.
- **Partner Profile Review**: Inspect delivery rating, historical completed deliveries, cancelled trips, and current availability status (`AVAILABLE` / `ON_TRIP`).
- **Partner Access Control**: Ability to block or unblock riders to maintain farm reputation and quality handling.
