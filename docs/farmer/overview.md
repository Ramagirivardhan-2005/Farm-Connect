# Farmer Role — Channel Operations Overview

The **Farmer** role in FarmConnect provides producers with their own dedicated, brandable FarmConnect Channel. Farmers directly manage their farm identity, crop inventory, incoming orders, packing workflow, delivery fleet, promotional offers, and revenue analytics.

## Core Farmer Capabilities
1. **Channel Ownership**: Branded profile with custom banner, farm biography, social media links, APEDA/NPOP organic certifications, and agricultural achievements.
2. **AI-Assisted Item Management**: List produce with crop categories, botanical types, and automated Gemini AI copywriting for descriptions and sensory taste profiles.
3. **Dynamic Visual Stock**: Manage available harvest stock with atomic inventory reservations that prevent overselling.
4. **Order State Machine Transitions**: Advance orders step-by-step from `PAID` to `ACCEPTED`, `PROCESSING`, `PACKED`, and `READY_FOR_PICKUP`.
5. **Delivery Partner Network**: Authorize and coordinate with delivery riders who claim orders once marked ready for pickup.
6. **Promotional Banners**: Submit harvest festival discounts to Super Admin for approval and channel broadcast.
7. **Direct Revenue Analytics**: View real-time gross revenue, daily earnings, and pending escrow payouts.
