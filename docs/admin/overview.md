# Super Admin Role — Overview & Platform Governance

Route: `/admin/dashboard`

The **Super Admin** possesses full administrative and regulatory authority across the FarmConnect marketplace ecosystem (Instruction 64).

## Governance Dashboards
- **System Metrics**: Total Users, Total Farmers, Active Channels, Total Harvests, Lifetime GMV Revenue, Active Delivery Fleet, and Pending KYC Verifications.
- **Farmer KYC & Land Verification (`/admin/verifications`)**: In-depth review of APEDA certifications, land titles, and penny-drop bank status.
- **Hero Content CMS (`/admin/hero-content`)**: Real-time management of marketing slides, headlines, and carousel sequences on authentication pages.
- **Promotional Banners Approval (`/admin/banners`)**: Review and approve festival discounts created by farmers.
- **Channel Regulatory Oversight (`/admin/channels`)**: Activate, suspend, or deactivate channels upon quality inspection.
- **Tamper-Evident Audit Logging (`/admin/audit-logs`)**: Security inspection of system-level actions and state transitions.
